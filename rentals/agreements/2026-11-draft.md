## 1. Who's who and what's rented

This agreement is between **Sprout Tote Company** ("we", "us") and the person booking ("you"). It covers the booking shown on your confirmation, identified by its booking reference.

If you book for a business or organization, you confirm you're allowed to, and "you" includes that business or organization.

- **Totes:** reusable plastic totes with lids (27-gallon, and any other sizes we offer). Each tote carries a label with a unique code; the totes remain our property.
- **Equipment:** any equipment listed on your booking, for example dollies, hand trucks, moving blankets or floor protection. Equipment remains our property and must be returned with the totes.
- **Supplies you buy:** anything listed on your booking as purchased, for example mattress bags or packing kits, is yours to keep.
- **Included with every rental:** color-coded labels, zip ties and a marker. These are yours to use and keep.

Your booking page lists exactly what you're renting, the dates and the price.

## 2. Rental period, dropoff and pickup

- **Rental period:** starts on your dropoff date and ends on your pickup date. Online bookings are in whole weeks, 2 weeks or longer; we may agree a different length with you in writing (for example, 1 week).
- **Dropoff:** we deliver to the dropoff address on your booking, by the time shown for your dropoff day.
- **Pickup:** we collect from the pickup address on your booking. It can be the same as the dropoff address (for example, an office move within one building or a donation drive). Have the totes, lids and equipment emptied, stacked and ready by the time shown for your pickup day.
- **Pickup address:** you must give it before dropoff. We won't deliver without it.
- **At the door:** you choose whether we leave items at the door or porch, or schedule a time when you'll be there. **If you choose door or porch, you accept the risk of loss or theft from that point until we collect them.**
- **Access:** make sure we can safely reach the dropoff and pickup spots. Tell us about gate codes, stairs, loading docks or parking in your booking notes.
- **Service area:** delivery is free within our standard area. Addresses farther out may have a delivery fee or be outside our service area. Our website and your booking show the area and any fee before you confirm.
- **Early pickup:** if you finish early, ask and we'll collect sooner when we can. Unused days aren't refunded.

## 3. Prices, payment and card authorization

- **Price:** the total shown on your booking (package, extra totes, rentals, items bought and any delivery fee), plus applicable Maryland sales tax. Prices are locked when you book.
- **Card on file:** you give us a payment card when you book. It's stored by our payment processor, Stripe; we never see or keep your full card number.
- **When you're charged:**
    - **Booking:** nothing is charged.
    - **About a day before dropoff:** we place a temporary hold for your total. Your bank may show it as pending.
    - **At dropoff:** the hold becomes the charge, adjusted for any changes to your booking.
- **If the hold is declined,** we'll email you to update your card. We may cancel the booking if we can't place a hold before dropoff.
- **Authorization for later charges.** By signing, you authorize us to charge the card on file, without asking again each time, for amounts due under this agreement:
    - extra rental weeks (section 4),
    - a late cancellation (section 5),
    - lost or damaged items (section 7),
    - any increase to your booking you request.

    We'll email you a receipt for every charge. You can update your card from your booking page.

## 4. Extensions when totes aren't ready

- **Grace period:** if the totes aren't ready when we arrive on pickup day, we'll come back later that same day if you let us know they're ready.
- **Automatic extension:** if we still can't collect everything on pickup day, your rental extends by **one week** and we charge your card for that week at the weekly extension price shown on your booking. This repeats each week until we collect.
- **Asking for more time:** you can ask to extend in advance from your booking page or by contacting us, at the same weekly price, if the totes aren't booked by someone else.

## 5. Changes and cancellations

- **Changes:** you can change dates, package, extras or addresses from your booking page up to **72 hours** before your dropoff time, if what you ask for is available. The new price replaces the old one.
- **Cancel 72 hours or more before dropoff:** free. Nothing is charged.
- **Cancel less than 72 hours before dropoff:** we charge **50% of the rental** (package, extra totes and rented equipment), plus tax on that amount. No delivery fee or purchased supplies are charged.
- **If we need to change or cancel:** we confirm bookings right away, but within **48 hours** of your booking we may ask you to change it (for example, a different day) or cancel it. If we cancel, you owe nothing. We may also cancel if you don't give a moving-in address or a working card before dropoff.

## 6. Using the totes and equipment

Use the totes and equipment only for packing, moving or temporarily storing belongings (yours or your organization's), and keep them at the dropoff or pickup address unless we agree otherwise.

**Don't put these in the totes:**

- hazardous materials: gasoline, propane, paint, solvents, chemicals, ammunition, fireworks, or anything flammable or corrosive
- food, liquids or anything that can leak, spoil or attract pests
- plants or soil, live animals
- anything illegal

**Please also:**

- don't overload a tote so it can't close or be lifted safely; about 50 lb per tote is a sensible limit
- don't write on, paint or put stickers on the totes or lids (use the labels we provide)
- don't remove or cover the tote's code label
- empty everything out before pickup; we're not responsible for items left inside
- use dollies and hand trucks as intended; you're responsible for using them safely

## 7. Loss, damage and theft

You're responsible for everything you rent from dropoff until we collect it, including loss or theft from a porch or doorstep. Normal wear (scuffs, scratches) is fine.

After pickup we clean and inspect each tote by its code. If anything is missing or damaged, we charge your card on file:

| Item | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Tote (with or without lid) missing, or broken beyond use | $10 each |
| Lid missing or broken (tote fine) | $5 each |
| Rented equipment missing or damaged | the replacement price for that item on our price list |

- **Timing:** we finish inspection within **3 days** of pickup. We won't charge for damage found later.
- **Notice:** we email you an itemized list with the tote codes before or when we charge.
- **Found later:** if a tote we charged for as missing comes back to us in usable shape, we'll refund its charge minus rent for the extra time you had it: $3 per tote for each week or part of a week from your original pickup date until we get it back. If that rent equals or exceeds the charge, there's no refund.

## 8. Our responsibilities and limits

- **What we do:** deliver clean, sound totes and working equipment on the agreed days, and collect them.
- **We're not a moving company.** We don't pack, carry or transport your belongings, and we don't insure them. Check your homeowner's or renter's insurance for coverage during your move.
- **Your belongings:** you pack and use the totes at your own risk. We're not responsible for loss of or damage to anything you put in them, unless it's caused by our own negligence.
- **If something we supplied fails:** tell us right away and we'll replace it if we can. [Our total responsibility to you for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for this booking], except where Maryland law doesn't allow that limit.
- **Delays:** we'll tell you as soon as we can if weather, emergencies or other events beyond our control delay a dropoff or pickup, and rebook at no extra cost.
- **Safety:** you're responsible for how you, your helpers and movers use the totes and equipment.

## 9. Tote labels, contents notes and your information

- **Contents notes:** you can scan a tote's QR code to note what's inside and which room it goes to. Only people with your private booking link can see these notes; anyone else who scans a tote sees only our name. Share the link only with people you trust.
- **Deleting notes:** we delete your contents notes **30 days** after pickup. Print or save your list before then if you want to keep it.
- **What we keep:** your name, contact details, addresses, booking history, signature record and payment records (card details stay with Stripe). We use them to provide the rental, take payment, handle disputes and meet legal and tax obligations, and we don't sell them. See our privacy policy at [sprouttoteco.com/privacy].
